Thanh Mich LTD ("we", "us", "our") develops WheelDecider (the "App"), an Android app for spinning a customizable decision wheel, playing three light-weight mini-games, and collecting cosmetic items with in-game virtual coins. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App and the services it uses collect, why, and how you can control it.

This policy describes WheelDecider as built, including the advertising and analytics software development kits (SDKs) already included in the app, whether or not ads are turned on for your particular version.

1. Data that never leaves your device

The following is stored only in the App's private storage on your device, using Android's local DataStore/preferences mechanism. We do not operate a server that receives it, and it is not shared with any third party:

  • Your wheels — names, options, and chosen colors that you create.
  • Your game progress — rank, virtual coins earned by playing, and any collectible items you've unlocked.
  • Your avatar photo, if you choose to set one. You pick it using the Android system photo picker, which does not require the App to ask for storage/photos permission; the selected image is copied into the App's private folder on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • App preferences — theme, text size, and sound/music on-off settings.

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes all of the above (see our Data Deletion page).

3. Advertising identifier and your choices

On Android, apps that show ads or measure installs typically use the Advertising ID (or, on newer Android versions, the Privacy Sandbox attribution APIs) instead of anything that identifies you personally by name. You can reset this identifier or opt out of personalized ads at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by Android version and OEM). Where legally required (e.g., in the EEA/UK), the consent dialog described above lets you choose whether these SDKs are allowed to use your data for advertising purposes at all.

5. Virtual currency

Coins earned in the wheel-spin and mining mini-games are entertainment-only virtual currency generated by the App. They are unrelated to any cryptocurrency, cannot be purchased, and cannot be exchanged for real money, goods, or any other asset.

6. Children's privacy

WheelDecider is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us using the details in section 9 and we will take appropriate action.

7. Data retention

On-device data (section 1) stays on your device until you delete it yourself or uninstall the App. Data collected by the third-party services in section 2 is retained according to each provider's own retention policies; we do not separately store copies of it.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data associated with you, or to object to certain processing. Because most App data lives only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights yourself, at any time, by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it, and by using your device's Ad Settings to reset/opt out of the advertising identifier. For anything else — including requests about data held by our third-party providers that you can't resolve yourself — contact us using section 9, or see our Data Deletion page for step-by-step instructions.
