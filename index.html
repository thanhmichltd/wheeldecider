Make a wheel

Your options, your colours, ready in a minute.

Add as many options as you want, duplicate one with a tap, and give each slice its own colour. The preview above the form redraws as you type, so you see the wheel before you save it.

Pick from 14 palettes grouped by mood, preview the whole wheel in that palette, then apply — or cancel and keep what you had.

● Live preview · duplicate option · per-slice colour
Spin it

One tap, one answer, no ceremony.

Tap the hub or the Spin button and the wheel eases to a stop under the needle. The result comes back on a ticket card you can dismiss, or spin again straight away.

The last result stays on screen while you decide what to do with it, instead of disappearing the moment the dialog closes.

● Result ticket · spin again · last result kept

FAQ
Is WheelDecider really free?

Yes. There is no Premium plan, no coin packs and no purchase screen. Everything opens by playing.

Can I buy coins, or cash them out?

No. Coins are virtual, earned in the app only. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought, sold or withdrawn — this is not a gambling app.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in of any kind. Open the app and start.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Wheels, the four games and the shop all run on the device.

Where are my wheels stored?

On your phone. The wheels and options you type are not uploaded.

Does it show ads?

This version does not. If advertising is added in a later update, it will sit in a few fixed places and never interrupt a spin.
