Data Deletion

Effective date: September 23, 2026 · Published by Thanh Mich LTD

Privacy Policy Terms of Use Data Deletion Support

WheelDecider does not use accounts or sign-in — you can start using the App immediately, and everything you create (wheels, game progress, avatar photo, preferences) is stored only on your own device. This page explains how to delete that data, and how to control the identifiers used by the third-party services described in our Privacy Policy.

Delete your data on this device

Your wheels, coins, collected items, and avatar photo are stored in the App's private storage. To delete them:

  1. Clear the App's storage — Android Settings → Apps → WheelDecider → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This removes all wheels, progress, and your avatar photo immediately, while keeping the App installed.
  2. Or uninstall the App — this removes the App and all of its locally stored data from your device at the same time.

Neither of these actions requires contacting us, because this data was never sent to us in the first place.

Delete data held by third-party services

The App uses advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, and attribution SDKs listed in our Privacy Policy (section 2), some of which may associate activity with your device's advertising identifier. To limit or reset that:

  • Go to your device's Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads, and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID / Opt out of ads personalization (wording varies by Android version and OEM).
  • Where legally required, you can also change your choice in the in-app consent dialog the next time it is shown.

Requesting deletion directly from us

If you have a request we haven't covered above — for example about data a third-party provider retained on our behalf — email us and describe your request. We will respond within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days.
